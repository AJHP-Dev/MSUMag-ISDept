☁️ Google App Sync

Ready — never synced yet

Sync the whole scheduler through a Google Apps Script web app. Your data survives browser clears and syncs across devices. The same endpoint powers the student QR portal in Resources → Add student.

Setup (2 minutes):
  1. Go to script.google.com → New project.
  2. Click Copy Google Apps Script setup in the Patcher card below (or copy from the previous version's prompt) and paste it into Code.gs.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app. Execute as Me, access Anyone.
  4. Copy the /exec URL, paste it above, click Save config, then Test connection.
  5. Turn on Auto-save so every edit goes to Google. The student QR portal link in Resources → Add student automatically carries this endpoint.

🩹 Patcher

2 patch(es)

Extend the app by pasting small JavaScript patches. Patches run after the app loads in an isolated context with access to ctx.getState(), ctx.save(), ctx.render(), ctx.toast() and ctx.pushSnapshot().

Workflow:
  1. Click Copy AI prompt — a ready-made prompt is copied to your clipboard.
  2. Paste it into DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Claude, etc., replace the placeholder line with what you want to add, and send.
  3. The AI replies with a // PATCH: … block. Paste it into the form below and save.
  4. The patch runs immediately after reload, and stays enabled until you disable or delete it.
